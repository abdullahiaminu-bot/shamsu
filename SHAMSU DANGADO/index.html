For authors

Make room
for your ideas.

We want the submission process to be clear and welcoming. Here's what to know before you send us your work.

Contact the editors ↗
01 What we publish

We consider original research articles, interdisciplinary essays, reviews, and proposals for dialogue. Submissions should offer a clear contribution and speak to readers beyond a single specialty.

02 Preparing your manuscript

Send an anonymized manuscript in an editable document format. Include a title, abstract of up to 250 words, 4–6 keywords, and references in a consistent citation style. Remove identifying details from the manuscript file.

03 Review & editorial process

Submissions are first assessed for fit and originality. Research articles that proceed are reviewed by independent subject specialists. Editors share a decision and constructive feedback with the corresponding author.

04 Originality & permissions

Work must be original and not under consideration elsewhere. Authors are responsible for securing permission to reproduce third-party material and for declaring any relevant conflicts of interest.

Our commitments

Clear standards.
Careful scholarship.

Trust is built through transparent editorial practice. These principles guide every stage of our work.

01

Peer review

Research submissions are evaluated through a fair, independent review process guided by subject expertise.

02

Research integrity

We expect honest reporting, proper attribution, and responsible handling of research data and methods.

03

Open dialogue

We welcome respectful debate and a diversity of perspectives, and do not tolerate harassment or discrimination.

04

Corrections

When the record needs to be corrected, we investigate concerns and publish updates transparently.
